Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Applies to this static website only

CodeLibrary is a free, independent reading website. This page describes what the website code does today, not a promise about services that may be added later.

Information stored on your device

CodeLibrary uses your browser's localStorage to remember favorite book IDs, chapter/section positions, and the preferred display language. This information stays in your browser and is not submitted to CodeLibrary by this site. If you clear your browser's storage, these preferences disappear.

External book providers

When you open an online book, your browser requests its text from GitHub's raw content service or the jsDelivr CDN. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request time according to their own policies. Opening a source link sends you to that external website. CodeLibrary is not affiliated with these providers.

Audio and browser features

The Listen feature uses your browser or device's speech synthesis feature. Its behavior and any data handling depend on your browser, operating system, and installed voice services. CodeLibrary does not create or upload audio recordings through this feature.

Accounts, ads, and analytics

This version contains no user accounts, newsletter signups, payment processing, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts. It does not set cookies from its JavaScript. Your hosting provider may separately log visits and other technical information; its practices must be reviewed and disclosed by the site owner.

Children and learning

Anyone can use the reading activities without registering. CodeLibrary does not intentionally request names, ages, or other identifying information from children. Third-party providers and hosts may have their own policies.

Changes and questions

If new features such as accounts, analytics, donations, or forms are added, this policy should be updated before they go live. For privacy concerns, visit the .

Why CodeLibrary exists

Reading is learning.
Learning is reading.

A home for curious readers, creative thinkers, and the next generation of storytellers.

CodeLibrary is a free, code-inspired reading space that connects literature and technology. Classic stories become readable lines of code, inviting readers to explore chapters, experiment with programming-language styles, discover authors, listen to words come alive, and practice with original study guides.

We believe every page can teach us something new—and every line can start a journey. You do not need to know programming to enjoy a book here.

How it works

  • Search by book title or author, or type simple commands into the Explorer or sidebar.
  • Read in Python, JavaScript, Ruby, or C++ inspired display modes. These are safe visual representations; no arbitrary code runs.
  • Jump chapters and save your place; mark books as favorites.
  • Use built-in browser text-to-speech for a listen-along experience on supported devices.
  • Practice vocabulary, book comprehension, and beginner programming concepts in the free Learning Lab and each reader study guide.

What is a static site?

It is a site that serves HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without a private application server. Visitors can read and use features in their browser, but they cannot upload books to the public collection: the upload feature has been removed. Adding published titles is handled by the website owner updating the source code and redeploying it.

For an owner-only uploading dashboard, accounts, synchronized favorites, or a true audiobook streaming service, you would need a secure backend and user authentication.
